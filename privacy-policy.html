Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 9, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Grupo SP ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with our internal WhatsApp messaging application. This application is used exclusively by Grupo SP to send WhatsApp notifications and communications to our customers.

This app is not available to the general public. It is an internal tool operated solely by Grupo SP staff.

2. Information We Use

To send WhatsApp messages to our customers, we may use the following information:

  • Customer phone numbers
  • Customer names (where applicable)
  • Business-related communication content (e.g. order updates, notifications)

This information is sourced directly from our own customer records and is used solely for the purpose of delivering messages through the WhatsApp Business API.

3. How We Use the Information

We use customer contact information exclusively to:

  • Send transactional or informational WhatsApp messages to customers who have an existing relationship with Grupo SP
  • Operate and maintain our WhatsApp Business messaging service

We do not use this information for any other purpose.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share customer data with any third parties, except as required to deliver messages through Meta's WhatsApp Business API, in accordance with WhatsApp's Business Policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain customer contact information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we handle against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at: gruposp.sistemas@hotmail.com
